01 Mandate

We buy to hold.

A holding company is a different instrument to a fund. There is no exit clock, no vintage year, no obligation to return capital on a schedule somebody else set.

That single structural fact shapes everything downstream — what we underwrite, how long we are willing to wait, and how closely we sit with the people actually running the business.

  1. i

    Permanent capital

    We are not obliged to sell. Time becomes an input to the return rather than a constraint on it.

  2. ii

    Concentration by conviction

    Enough positions to hold through a cycle. Few enough that each one is understood in full.

  3. iii

    Aligned interest

    We invest beside the operators, on the same terms, with incentives settled before the paperwork.
